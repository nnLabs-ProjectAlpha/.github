**Novus | Nexum Labs** builds AI infrastructure for teams that need AI they can prove, not just AI they can access. Our gateway, **V-Stela**, verifies, logs and defends every AI action inside the customer's own compliance and data boundaries.

This organization is the private engagement workspace for **ProjectAlpha**. Its repositories are available to invited partners and contractors only.

### The suite

| Product | Role |
|---|---|
| **V-Stela** | The foundation. Enforces policy, routes tool calls and preserves the evidence trail across every AI request. |
| **Lexicon** | The paper trail. Drafting, redlining, citation verification and defensible work product for legal teams. |
| **Functo** | The calculation layer. Validated scientific models for oil and gas, industrial operations and research. |
| **Itercrux IDE** | The agent floor. A governed environment for building multi-agent workflows. |
| **Spiro per Verbum** | The spoken record. Hands-free work, consent capture and auditable spoken workflows. |

# Code of conduct

Novus | Nexum Laboratories Inc. — nnLabs ProjectAlpha.

Everyone working in this organization, whether staff or third-party developer, is expected to:

- Be respectful and professional in issues, pull requests, reviews and every other channel.
- Give and accept review feedback about the work, not the person.
- Keep confidential information confidential, as set out in your agreements.
- Raise concerns early with a maintainer or the organization owner.

Harassment, discrimination, or sharing another person's private information is not tolerated and results in removal of access.

Report conduct concerns privately to the organization owner, Daniel Hill.
